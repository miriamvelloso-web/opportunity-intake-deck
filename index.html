    Prioritisation

    Scoring Framework

    Each intake opportunity is scored across 3 weighted dimensions. Strategic Impact thresholds are calibrated against actual annual impact data from P&T OKR Key Results (Q3–Q1 Y25–Y26). Scores range 1–5, producing a weighted total out of 5.0.

    Strategic Impact

    Weight: 50% • Calibrated from P&T OKR actuals (median €7.6M)
    ScoreCriteria
    5Annual impact > €15M or critical regulatory risk
    4Annual impact €7M–€15M or major compliance
    3Annual impact €3M–€7M or moderate impact
    2Annual impact €1M–€3M or narrow scope
    1Annual impact < €1M or not quantified

    Confidence Level

    Weight: 30%
    ScoreCriteria
    5Full BC with validated data / A/B tested
    4BC with solid financial projections
    3BC with estimates, some assumptions
    2Rough estimates, no formal BC
    1No data, conceptual only

    Business Priority

    Weight: 20%
    ScoreCriteria
    5Multi-market / regulatory / legal risk
    4Regional scale, multiple functions
    3One market, cross-functional
    2Single team scope
    1Sub-functional nice-to-have
